# Ormeq for Android 0.1.3-android.1

An Android-only update: the game is still Ormeq 0.1.3, as on Windows. It
brings the menus' controls to every list, as a player asked.

- **Every list has a highlighted choice.** Item lists (wear, read, throw,
  drop and the rest), spell lists, shops and a building's services now work
  like the menus: one choice is highlighted, the d-pad's up and down or a
  drag move it, and **OK** takes it. Tapping a choice still takes it at once,
  now anywhere along its row.
- **Shops:** **Buy** buys the highlighted item and **Info** describes it.
  Moving past the last item turns to the next page of stock, which could not
  be reached by touch before.
- Lists that already highlight their own choice, such as the quest journal,
  are unchanged.

Gameplay and saves are unchanged. It installs over 0.1.3 and earlier
releases and keeps your characters.

## Ormeq for Android 0.1.3

The game is Ormeq 0.1.3, as on Windows (its
[release notes](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq/blob/v0.1.3/RELEASE_NOTES.md)),
with a choice of d-pad.

- From Ormeq 0.1.3: **Options and Help are on the Menu button.** The pause
  menu now has the game's whole Options menu and the help index, which were
  only under More.
- **A square d-pad, if you prefer a numpad:** turn on **s_deco** in **Menu >
  Settings > Touch** (or **s_deco (square/Numpad shaped)** in **Menu >
  Options > Input**). The d-pad becomes nine keys, three by three, laid out
  like a keyboard's numpad, with an arrow on each direction and Wait in the
  middle. A press takes the key it lands on. In a menu only its up and down
  keys work, and when aiming it turns orange with fire in the middle. The game
  remembers your choice for every character, and the start screen shows it
  too.

Gameplay and saves are unchanged. 0.1.3 installs over 0.1.2 and earlier
releases and keeps your characters.

## Ormeq for Android 0.1.1-android.2

An Android-only update: the game is still Ormeq 0.1.1, as on Windows. It
brings two changes to the touch controls that a player asked for.

- **Dragging in a menu or list moves the highlight with your finger.** Drag
  up and the highlight goes up; drag down and it goes down, as the d-pad's
  up and down move it. Before, it went the opposite way, as if the drag were
  scrolling text. This covers the menus, character creation (including the
  summary and stats pages, where a drag highlights a row), the option and
  knowledge lists, the quest journal's list and the Past Reckonings list.
  On help and other pages of text, a drag still scrolls the text with your
  finger.
- **One direction per press on the d-pad.** A press keeps the direction it
  started on until you lift your finger: sliding your thumb no longer turns
  your character, so a drifting thumb takes no step you did not mean. Holding
  still walks, in that one direction. A press on the centre (Wait) that
  slides off it is cancelled: no wait, and no step.

Gameplay and saves are unchanged. It installs over 0.1.1-android.1, 0.1.1
or 0.1.0 and keeps your characters.

## Ormeq for Android 0.1.1-android.1

An Android-only update: the game is still Ormeq 0.1.1, as on Windows, and
the next Windows version brings the two version numbers back together.

- **Every menu has a confirmation button.** Menus and lists, from the start
  screen and character creation to the pause menu and Settings, always show
  one choice highlighted, starting on your current one. **OK** takes it, and
  the d-pad's up and down move the highlight. Tapping a choice still takes it
  at once. Before, race and class lists had no OK, and the pause menu's and
  Settings' OK did nothing until a drag had highlighted a row.
- The d-pad in a menu has only up and down, and it never covers a choice: it
  shrinks to fit beside the list, or hides where a long list leaves no room
  on a small phone (OK, tapping and dragging still work there).
- On the summary and stats pages of character creation, OK still goes on,
  as their footers say. On help and other pages of text, OK now closes the
  page.
- The start screen shows the whole version, 0.1.1-android.1.

Gameplay and saves are unchanged. It installs over 0.1.1 or 0.1.0 and keeps
your characters.

## Ormeq for Android 0.1.1

Character creation fixes, with the game at Ormeq 0.1.1 as on Windows:

- Character creation's touch buttons now offer only what each page does. On
  the first page, the type of game, a choice is made by tapping it, and OK did
  nothing there, so the game looked stuck
  ([issue 1](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq-Android/issues/1)). OK
  now appears only where a page goes on or takes the highlighted race or
  class; a page that is a list to choose from has Back as its large button;
  Random and Info appear only where they work. The first page now says to
  tap a type of game.
- From Ormeq 0.1.1: highlights no longer carry from one character creation
  page to the next, Quick Start and the Random patron can be chosen only
  where they are shown, and Return makes its accept sound only where a page
  acts on it.

Gameplay and saves are unchanged. 0.1.1 installs over 0.1.0 and keeps your
characters.

## Ormeq for Android 0.1.0

First Android release.

The game is Ormeq 0.1.0, the same version as the Windows release, whose
[release notes](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq/blob/v0.1.0/RELEASE_NOTES.md)
describe it in full: FrogComposband's character choices and dungeons in a
world shared with Ammerow: Lands Beyond, the quest journal and questgiver
cards, The Reckoner, ASCII, Hybrid and Full Tile maps, and sound effects,
ambience and title music. This edition plays it by touch.

- A rail of screens down the left edge (Menu, Inventory, Equipment, More,
  Quest, Character and Log), a d-pad that walks while held, with Wait at its
  centre, and a cluster of four buttons that offers what can be done now.
- The cluster follows the focus: the answers to the game's question, an item's
  own actions, a targeted monster's attacks, or what can be done on your
  square. Its buttons keep their places; only their labels change.
- An item tapped in Inventory or Equipment offers exactly the commands whose
  own rules accept it. Fire appears for a launcher with ammunition it can
  shoot, and a character with spells and a launcher keeps Fire beside Cast
  while a hostile monster is in view.
- Look cards beside the creature's place on the map, with Next, Prev, Target
  and sideways swipes.
- Quick slots for items, spells and commands, kept with each character, and
  Again.
- Taps travel, aim and choose rows; holding on the map shows a creature's card;
  two fingers zoom the map; Android's Back is Escape.
- The quest journal, questgiver cards (Accept, Later, Continue) and Past
  Reckonings work by touch, with their help worded for it. The game's keyboard
  hints are reworded for touch wherever they are drawn.
- The on-screen keyboard for names, numbers and other text; a hardware
  keyboard works as on Windows.
- Landscape on phones. Tablets and unfolded foldables can be held either way;
  folding and split screen resize the game as it runs. The map runs under the
  rail to the edges of the screen.
- The character is saved whenever Android sends the game to the background,
  including in the middle of a prompt, and a failed save is reported.
- Music and ambience are compressed to keep the APK small. With the ambience
  off, the audio device sleeps between sounds.
- A help page on battery life: what the ambience and the weather cost.

## Platform and installation

Android 8.0 or later, on a 64-bit device (arm64-v8a, or x86_64). Install the
APK as [PLAYING.md](PLAYING.md) describes. Later official releases are signed
with the same key, so they install over this one and keep your characters.

This release was tested on an emulated Android 17 phone. Development builds
were also tested on emulated phones, tablets and foldables, and played on an
Android 9 tablet.

## Known limitations

- Not available from an app store: the APK is installed directly.
- 32-bit-only devices are not supported.
- Characters cannot yet be copied out of the app, to another device or to and
  from the Windows edition, except by Android's own backup and device
  transfer.
- Future releases may change save compatibility, as on Windows.
