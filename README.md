# Ormeq for Android

**A free, single-player roguelike in a country built from stolen worlds, made to be played by touch.**

Nobody built this place. It was collected. Beneath its towns lie places taken
from elsewhere: courts, drowned cities, mountains and worse. Going deeper
means travelling further into what each place once was.

Choose a character, prepare in town, and venture into dangerous dungeons.
Find strange equipment, learn your enemies, and decide when to press on or
get out alive. Ormeq combines FrogComposband's deep character and combat
systems with a modern SDL3 interface and its own setting, shared with
**Ammerow: Lands Beyond**. On Android it is the same game as on Windows,
version for version, with touch controls of its own.

## Take your time. Make it count.

- **Build a character worth risking.** Explore a wide range of races, classes,
  magic and equipment combinations inherited from FrogComposband.
- **Think through each turn.** Nothing moves until you do. Positioning,
  supplies, resistances and knowing when to retreat matter, and traditional
  roguelike permadeath makes survival count.
- **Explore beyond the next room.** Travel between towns, take on quests and
  descend through procedurally generated dungeons.
- **Make the view your own.** Choose ASCII, Hybrid or Full Tile mode, pinch to
  zoom the map, and adjust the theme. Sound effects, ambient soundscapes and
  title music are included in the APK, with separate audio controls.

## Made for touch

Every control presses the game's own keys, so nothing is lost on the way to
your fingers. The buttons at the bottom right offer what can be done now:
Get, Down or Open where you stand, the answers when the game asks, Fire or
Cast when a monster is in view. Tap an item and its own actions appear,
decided by the game's rules. Tap the map to travel there, hold the d-pad to
walk, and keep favourite potions, spells and commands in quick slots. A
hardware keyboard works as it does on Windows.

## Tiles or characters: your choice

Switch to Enhanced Square ASCII for crisp, readable layouts, or combine
character glyphs with artwork. Full Tile mode also offers **Keep ASCII floors
only**. Missing artwork always falls back to a glyph.

## Understand the encounter

Tap **Look** to see a creature's card beside its place on the map. Cards bring
together its portrait, what your character has learned and its description.
Unknown statistics stay unknown; swipe to the next creature in view.

[![A scrawny cat's card beside its place in Outpost: a character-art portrait, with its armour, hit points and attacks still unknown](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq-Android/releases/download/v0.1.0/ormeq-android-05-creature-card.png)](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq-Android/releases/download/v0.1.0/ormeq-android-05-creature-card.png)

Curious about the numbers? **The Reckoner** explains recorded combat with
actual dice rolls and a visual trail through the calculation. Its live card is
optional. Tap it, or open **Log**, for **Past Reckonings**, where you can
revisit recorded results and inspect the working. It never changes the rolls,
reveals hidden inputs or presents a partial calculation as complete.

[![Past Reckonings explains an arrow that dealt 20 damage to a scrawny cat: dice of 3, 3 and 2, then the launcher's multiplier](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq-Android/releases/download/v0.1.0/ormeq-android-06-reckoner.png)](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq-Android/releases/download/v0.1.0/ormeq-android-06-reckoner.png)

Meet the people offering work, read the objective and directions before you
accept, and keep track of the journey in the **Quest journal**, one tap away on
the left. Active and completed lists, reward receipts and failure records help
you pick up where you left off.

## Start playing

1. On your phone or tablet,
   [download the APK](https://github.com/EliteIntegrity/Ormeq-Android/releases/download/v0.1.1-android.2/Ormeq-0.1.1-android.2.apk).
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install
   apps, then choose **Install**. Nothing else needs installing.
3. Open **Ormeq** and choose **New character**. **Menu**, top left, has the
   settings; **More** has every command, help included.

A newer official APK installs over this one and keeps your characters.
Uninstalling Ormeq deletes them.
